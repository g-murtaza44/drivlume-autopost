The EV crown changed hands. 👑

In 2025 BYD sold about 2.26 million battery-electric cars. Tesla delivered 1.64 million.

That made BYD the world's biggest seller of fully electric cars for the first time. Counting plug-in hybrids, BYD sold about 4.6 million vehicles.
