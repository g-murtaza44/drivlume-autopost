Tough year in Zuffenhausen, but not for the 911. 📊

Porsche delivered 178,532 cars from January to September 2026, 16% fewer than a year earlier. The Cayenne was the top seller with 59,586.

The 911 bucked the trend: 42,217 delivered, up 12%.
