America came to the Green Hell. 🇺🇸

In 2025 the Chevrolet Corvette ZR1X lapped the Nürburgring Nordschleife in 6:49.275, the fastest time for an American production car at that point.

The ZR1X pairs a twin-turbo V8 with an electric front axle for well over 1,000 hp.
