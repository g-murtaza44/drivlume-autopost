Where Porsche's Le Mans legend began. 🏆

On 14 June 1970 Hans Herrmann and Richard Attwood won Le Mans in the #23 Porsche 917 KH, Porsche's first overall victory there. In a rain-hit race they covered 343 laps and 4,607.811 km.

The 917 made about 580 hp.
