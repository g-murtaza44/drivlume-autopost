The hot hatch king is back. 🏁

In 2026 the VW Golf GTI Edition 50 lapped the Nürburgring Nordschleife in 7:44, taking the front-wheel-drive production car record from the Honda Civic Type R.

The Edition 50 celebrates 50 years of the GTI (born 1976).
