Meet the new Elantra. 🆕

Hyundai gave the 2027 Elantra and 2028 Elantra Hybrid their global debut at the LA Auto Show on 19 Nov 2026. The petrol car has a 2.0-litre with 147 hp, the hybrid makes 155 hp combined, and the cabin gets a 12.9-inch touchscreen.

Prices are not out yet.
