Nine years after it was announced, it showed up. 🚀

On 15 October 2026 Tesla unveiled the production prototype of the new Roadster in Texas. Tesla claims 0-60 mph in under 1 second and over 370 km/h, with production planned for 2028.

The original Roadster, built from 2008, was Tesla's very first car.
