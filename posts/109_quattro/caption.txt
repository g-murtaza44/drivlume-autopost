The car that changed rallying. 🏁

Audi unveiled the quattro at the Geneva show in March 1980: a 2.1-litre turbo five-cylinder with 200 hp and permanent four-wheel drive.

Audi says 11,452 were built until 1991, plus 224 short-wheelbase Sport quattros.
