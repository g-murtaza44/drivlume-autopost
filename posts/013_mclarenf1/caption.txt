First time at Le Mans. First place. 🏁

In June 1995 the McLaren F1 GTR won the 24 Hours of Le Mans outright, at its very first attempt.

The winning car was driven by JJ Lehto, Yannick Dalmas and Masanori Sekiya, with a BMW V12 behind them.
