Best car in the world? The judges said yes. 🏆

On 1 April 2026 at the New York Auto Show, the electric BMW iX3 won World Car of the Year 2026 and World Electric Vehicle 2026.

It's the first model of BMW's Neue Klasse and beat the Hyundai Palisade and Nissan Leaf in the final.
