A yellow tribute to a JDM legend. 💛

The 2027 Acura Integra Type S Heritage Edition, announced on 13 Nov 2026, wears Blazing Phoenix Yellow and is limited to 700 cars for North America. It has a 320 hp turbo 2.0-litre and costs $58,295 with destination.

It celebrates 30 years of the DC2 Integra Type R, which made 195 hp.
