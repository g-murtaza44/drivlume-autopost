Built for one thing: winning rallies. 🏆

The wedge-shaped Lancia Stratos HF was designed from scratch for rallying. Lancia won the World Rally Championship manufacturers' title in 1974, 1975 and 1976 (the 1974 title shared with the Fulvia).
