Six years in a row. Nobody sells more cars than Toyota. 🌍

Toyota Group sold a record 11.3 million vehicles in 2025, the world's top-selling automaker for the sixth straight year.

Hybrids made up 42% of Toyota's own sales. Volkswagen Group came second with about 9 million.
