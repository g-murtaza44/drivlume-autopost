The Countach's wild successor. 😈

The Lamborghini Diablo was unveiled in January 1990. Its 5.7-litre V12 made 492 hp, and Lamborghini claimed a 325 km/h top speed.

It was the first Lamborghini able to go beyond 320 km/h.
