Range anxiety? Never heard of it. 🔋

On 5-6 July 2025 a Lucid Air Grand Touring drove 1,205 km from St. Moritz, Switzerland to Munich, Germany on a single charge.

Guinness World Records confirmed it as the longest journey by an electric car on one charge. That's about Karachi to Multan, without charging.
