The car many still call the most beautiful ever made. 🐆

The E-Type launched at the Geneva Motor Show on 15 March 1961 with a 3.8-litre straight-six making 265 bhp. Autocar recorded 150.4 mph in a test car.

It cost £2,097 as a Roadster, about half the price of rival Ferraris and Astons, and stayed in production for 14 years.
