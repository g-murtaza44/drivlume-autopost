A phone company built a Ring record car. 📱

In 2025 the production Xiaomi SU7 Ultra lapped the Nürburgring Nordschleife in 7:04.957, a record for electric executive cars at the time.

Porsche's Taycan Turbo GT took the record back in May 2026.
