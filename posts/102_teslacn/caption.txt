Tesla's China sales hit a 4-year low. 📉

CPCA data shows Tesla's October 2026 retail sales in China fell 66% year on year to 8,817 cars.

Exports from Giga Shanghai went the other way: up 66% to 59,012, a record 87% of the cars it wholesaled.
