Under 7 minutes, fully electric. 🟢

On 22 August 2025 the Yangwang U9 Xtreme lapped the Nürburgring Nordschleife in 6:59.157, the fastest lap for a production electric car at the time.

A few weeks later it also hit 496.22 km/h.
