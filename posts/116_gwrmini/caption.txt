Parallel parking, but make it impossible. 🤯

On Guinness World Records Day (19 Nov 2026) stunt driver Alastair Moffatt set the record for the tightest parallel park driving a car on two wheels, in a Mini Cooper, with a 6.53 m gap.

He and his 11-year-old son also set the tightest mirrored parallel park: 210 cm.
