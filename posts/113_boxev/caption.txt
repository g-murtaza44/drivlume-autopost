The Boxster goes electric next year. ⚡

Porsche says test cars have covered nearly 4 million km in more than 20 countries. The top model will do 0-60 mph in under 3 seconds on an 800-volt system, and Porsche calls it the most powerful 718 ever.

The world premiere is planned for Q1 2027, with production in Zuffenhausen.
