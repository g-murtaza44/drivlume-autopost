A 4-door electric family car. Under 7 minutes at the Ring. 🤯

On 7 May 2026 a Porsche Taycan Turbo GT with Weissach Package and the new Manthey Kit lapped the Nordschleife in 6:55.5, the record for electric executive cars.

It beat the Xiaomi SU7 Ultra's 7:04.957 by more than 9 seconds.
