One car. One legend reborn. 🏎️

Porsche showed the 911 GT3 Tribute to 911 GT1 in Guangzhou on 25 Nov 2026. Porsche says it is the world's only example. Its 4.0-litre flat-six makes 510 PS, revs to 9,000 rpm and does 0-100 km/h in 3.4 s.

It marks 30 years since the 911 GT1 debuted in 1996.
