The new Ferrari hypercar has arrived. 🐎

In October 2024 Ferrari revealed the F80, following the GTO, F40, F50, Enzo and LaFerrari.

A V6 hybrid with 1,200 cv, the most powerful road Ferrari ever. Only 799 will be built.
