Ford is going back to Le Mans for the win. 🏁

Revealed at Ford Field in Detroit on Thanksgiving Day, the GT41 runs a naturally aspirated 5.4-litre V8 based on the Coyote, plus a hybrid system. The name comes from its height: 41 inches to the air intake.

It races from the 2027 WEC opener in Qatar. Ford won Le Mans overall four years in a row, 1966 to 1969.

Can Ford beat Ferrari and Toyota at Le Mans again?
