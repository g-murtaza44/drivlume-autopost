An electric car from China just out-ran Bugatti. ⚡

On 14 September 2025 the Yangwang U9 Xtreme (BYD's luxury brand) reached 496.22 km/h at the ATP Papenburg test track in Germany.

That beat the 490.48 km/h run of the Bugatti Chiron Super Sport 300+ from 2019. Only 30 U9 Xtremes will be made.

Is the hypercar era now electric? 👇
