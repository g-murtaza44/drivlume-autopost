A Bronco with 1,200 horsepower. ❄️

Shown at SEMA on 3 November 2026, the Frostwarden is a one-off Arctic rescue build on 42-inch tyres and portal axles.

Its Megazilla 2.0 crate V8 wears a 3.0-litre Whipple supercharger and makes over 1,000 lb-ft. Ford calls it its most powerful crate engine, and the engine is for sale.
