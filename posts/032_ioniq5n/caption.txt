An electric hatch with fake gear shifts. And it won. ⚡

The Hyundai Ioniq 5 N won World Performance Car 2025. Its 'N e-shift' even simulates gear changes and engine sound.

In 2026 its sibling, the Ioniq 6 N, won the same award.
