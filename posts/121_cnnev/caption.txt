CPCA data released on 10 Nov 2026 shows new-energy vehicles (EVs and plug-in hybrids) took a record 67.5% of China's passenger car retail sales in October: about 1.11 million of 1.64 million.

The overall market fell 27% year on year. BYD led with 250,301 NEV sales.
