The last Jesko has left the building. 🏁

In November 2026 Koenigsegg handed over the 125th and final Jesko, a one-off spec called the Jesko 125. On E85 its twin-turbo V8 makes up to 1,600 hp.

Koenigsegg says the Jesko holds the 0-400-0 km/h record at 25.21 seconds. No direct successor has been confirmed.
