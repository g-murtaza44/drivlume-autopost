Aston's fastest open-top car yet. 💚

Revealed at the end of October 2026 in Las Vegas, the Valhalla Spider pairs a twin-turbo V8 with three electric motors for 1,064 hp. Aston Martin says it does 0-100 km/h in 2.5 seconds and 350 km/h with the roof on.

Deliveries are due in Q3 2027.
