The next M3 has four motors. 🔋

On 20 Nov 2026 BMW shared early details: one motor per wheel, an estimated 625 kW (about 850 hp), an 800-volt battery over 100 kWh and around 650 km of range. BMW says charging above 400 kW adds about 350 km in 10 minutes.

All figures are provisional.
