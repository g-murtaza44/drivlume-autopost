Ford went to the Green Hell and went very fast. 🏁

On 2 April 2026 Ford announced the GT Mk IV lapped the Nürburgring Nordschleife in 6:15.977 with Frédéric Vervisch. Ford says it is the fastest lap ever by a car with only a combustion engine.

Only 67 will be built, a nod to Le Mans 1967.

Could a road car ever beat it? 👀
