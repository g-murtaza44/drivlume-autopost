Croatia built a rocket. 🚀

In 2025 the Rimac Nevera R reached 431.45 km/h, a new top-speed record for electric production cars at the time.

Later that year the Yangwang U9 went even faster.

Rimac or Bugatti? (Fun fact: Rimac now runs Bugatti too.) 👇
