Godzilla's last roar. 🦖

On 26 August 2025 the final R35 Nissan GT-R rolled off the line at Nissan's Tochigi plant in Japan.

It was in production for 18 years (since 2007), with about 48,000 built. The last one: a Premium edition T-Spec in Midnight Purple.

Nissan says the GT-R name will return. Should it? 👇
