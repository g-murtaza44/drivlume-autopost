The Nevera era is over. ⚡

On 12 Nov 2026 Rimac said Nevera production stops at 71 cars: 51 Nevera and 20 Nevera R. The plan was 150 and 40. The last cars are due by the end of 2026.

The Nevera R set a 431.45 km/h top speed record, and Mate Rimac calls the Nevera the best-selling electric hypercar in the world.
