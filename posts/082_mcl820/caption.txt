McLaren has a new name game. 🧡

At the end of October 2026 McLaren announced the McL 820, successor to the 750S, due in 2027. McLaren says it will make 820 PS from the first engine it has designed fully in-house.

The 750S makes 750 PS, so that is a 70 PS jump.
