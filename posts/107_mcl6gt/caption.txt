McLaren brought back the stick shift. 🧡

The McL 6GT made its public debut at the Concours at Wynn Las Vegas in early November 2026. It's a rear-drive V8 with a carbon monocoque, hydraulic steering and a manual gearbox, the first manual McLaren since the F1.

Production starts in 2028.
