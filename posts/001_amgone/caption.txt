F1 engine. Number plate. 6:29 around the Green Hell. 🟢

In November 2024 the Mercedes-AMG ONE lapped the Nürburgring Nordschleife in 6:29.090, setting the lap record for road-legal production cars.

It uses a 1.6-litre hybrid V6 taken almost straight from Mercedes' Formula 1 car. Yes, a road car.
