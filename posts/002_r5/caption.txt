A 1972 icon came back as an EV and won. 💛

The Renault 5 E-Tech Electric was named European Car of the Year 2025, a year after the Renault Scenic E-Tech won it.

The design is a modern take on the original Renault 5 from 1972.

Should more old classics come back as EVs? 👇
