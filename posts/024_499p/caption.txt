Three in a row at Le Mans. 🇮🇹

In June 2025 the #83 AF Corse Ferrari 499P (Kubica, Ye, Hanson) won the 24 Hours of Le Mans.

That made it three straight overall wins for the 499P: 2023, 2024 and 2025.
