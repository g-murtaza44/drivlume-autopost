Honda's racing division is turning up the Type R. 🔴

At SEMA on 3 November 2026 Honda showed the Civic Type R HRC Concept and said it is in development for the US. It adds a sharper front bumper, arch winglets and a big rear wing.

HRC performance parts go on sale in the US in March 2027. No power figure or launch date yet.
