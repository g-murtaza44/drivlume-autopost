No turbos. No hybrid. 240 mph. 🚀

On 31 March 1998 Andy Wallace drove a McLaren F1 to a two-way average of 240.1 mph (386.4 km/h) at Ehra-Lessien in Germany.

The car used a 6.1-litre naturally aspirated BMW V12 with 627 bhp.
