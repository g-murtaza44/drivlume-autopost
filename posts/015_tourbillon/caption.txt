Bugatti said goodbye to the W16 with something even crazier. 🐎

In June 2024 Bugatti revealed the Tourbillon, the Chiron's successor.

A naturally aspirated 8.3-litre V16 plus three electric motors: 1,800 PS combined. Only 250 will be built.
