The widowmaker turned 50. 🔴

The Porsche 911 Turbo (type 930) was shown at the Paris Motor Show in October 1974.

In 2024 Porsche celebrated 50 years of the 911 Turbo. Its wide hips and whale-tail spoiler became poster legends.
